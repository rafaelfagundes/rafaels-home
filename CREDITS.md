## Asset processing

The imported assets were processed with glTF Transform. Transforms were baked, models were scaled to real-world dimensions, and the vehicles and fridge were recoloured as described above. Meshoptimizer simplification produced the `_lod1` nodes. Textures were converted to WebP and capped at 1024 px, with normal and roughness maps capped at 512 px. Both GLBs use meshopt compression.

The procedural furniture and its geometry are original code.
